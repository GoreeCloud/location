# GoreeCloud Location — Privacy Policy

## Scope

GoreeCloud Location processes highly sensitive location information. This policy defines the project privacy boundary for the Development repository and future accepted deployments.

## Governing principles

- Precise location is private by default.
- Collection, retention, sharing, and recovery require explicit purpose and authorization.
- No advertising identifiers, behavioral advertising, or sale of location data.
- Administration does not automatically grant access to ordinary users' location histories.
- Every accepted sample has an explicit owning user and source device.
- Tracking pause and credential revocation are server-enforced.
- Raw precise coordinates must not appear in ordinary application logs.

## User control

Users must be able to understand and control applicable tracking, sharing, retention, export, and deletion behavior. Sharing must be scoped, revocable, and independently controllable for live and historical access.

Presentation-only privacy modes may reduce shoulder-surfing risk but do not delete or redact authoritative server data and do not replace operating-system screen protection.

## Authority boundaries

Privacy Shield remains authoritative for shared privacy policy and decision state. GoreeCloud Identity owns principal/account/session identity. Wardveil Security owns shared security/protection state. GoreeCloud Policy owns policy decisions.

GoreeCloud Maps may consume approved current-position capabilities but must not become a second personal location-history or tracking authority.

## Providers and integrations

Map, geocoding, routing, notification, synchronization, backup, and other integrations must minimize disclosed data and use explicit authorization. Direct cross-application database access is not permitted unless separately approved.

## Current status

Location is Forge/Development. Source tests and UI controls do not establish production privacy acceptance, anti-stalking acceptance, production sharing, provider acceptance, deployment, release, or Anchor qualification.
