## 2026-08-22 — Project specification established

GoreeCloud Location was established as an original GoreeCloud-owned location application and service for private location history, real-time device tracking, sharing, geofencing, trips, places, maps, Find My recovery, and location analytics.

The project direction separated real-time tracking from long-term historical location while presenting both through one first-party product. Dawarich, Traccar, and Google Timeline were treated as capability references rather than permanent architectural authorities.

## 2026-08-29 — Find My Development expansion

The project specification added a first-party Find My capability and a larger recovery/security architecture covering owner-scoped device state, recovery sessions, observation trust, offline-finding cryptography, relay behavior, network participation, anti-stalking controls, sharing grants, approximate-location protection, trusted-place assurance, theft-protection decisions, notification behavior, retention, deletion, portability, observability, threat-model requirements, standards strategy, accessibility, and Stable-state gates.

These additions defined required product scope and Development checkpoints; they did not by themselves establish production or Stable acceptance.
